Indicators of Climate Change Over Time

Trends in Number of Natural Disasters

Hover your cursor over the graph to see exact counts for each year.

About the Data

Data was sourced from EM-DAT and then grouped by region and year. Although data for 2025 is available, we chose to exclude it because the year is not yet over so any conclusions could be misleading. This subset of data was then visualized using Plotly Express.

Significance

There are many fluctuations in the number of climate events each year, but the America's have experienced a net increase in the number of events from 2000 to 2024. In addition, the Americas remain high on the graph, with more natural disasters than Oceania, Africa, or Europe. Let's look more speficically at the United States by looking at the number of billion-dollar climate disasters over time.

Trends in High-Cost Events

Hover your cursor over the graph to see exact counts for each year.

About the Data

Data was sourced from NOAA and subsetted to only display disasters in 2024. It was then displayed by type of disaster using Plotly Express.

Significance

Billion-dollar events are instances in which the resulting costs and damages from a climate disaster exceed one billion U.S. dollars, including CPI adjustment for 2024. As shown by the line chart, there is an upward trend in the number of these billion-dollar events from 1980 to 2024. This indicates a potential increase in the severity and economic impact of climate-related disasters over time.

Indicators of Climate Change Over Time

Trends in Number of Natural Disasters

There are many fluctuations in the number of climate events each year, but the America's have experienced a net increase in the number of events from 2000 to 2024. In addition, the Americas remain high on the graph, with more natural disasters than Oceania, Africa, or Europe. Let's look more speficically at the United States by looking at the number of billion-dollar climate disasters over time.

Trends in High-Cost Events

The number of billion-dollar climate disasters in the United States has increased significantly over the past few decades. From the 1980s to the 2020s, there is a clear upward trend in the frequency of these costly events, indicating that climate change is likely contributing to more severe weather patterns and disasters.

How much do Natural Disasters Cost?

Tropical Cyclones Cost the U.S. $123.96 Billion in 2024

About the Data

Data was sourced from NOAA and subsetted to only look at 2024. "CPI-adjusted cost" accounts for inflation and was provided by the NOAA data.

Significance

Tropical cycles were the most expensive type of disaster in 2024, alongside causing the most deaths. These two factors place tropical cyclones as a high priority for climate change mitigation efforts.

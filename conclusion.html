Conclusions

By exploring these visualizations, we have established that climate-related disasters are an increasing threat not only globally, but also to the United States. In the U.S. specifically, tropical cyclones are the deadliest and costliest of all disaster types, despite not being the most prevalent type. However, the Geofluid Fluid Dynamics Laboratory of the NOAA reports that tropical cyclones are worsening as a result of climate warming. It is due to anthropogenic activity that tropical cyclone rainfall rates and intensities are increasing. In addition, a larger proportion of tropical cyclones are reaching intense Category 4 and 5 status due to human activity causing our planet to warm.

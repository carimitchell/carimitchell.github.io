What climate events occurred in 2024?

Storms and Floods Made up the Majority of Global Events

Hover your cursor over the graph to see the count and percentage for each disaster type.

About the Data

Data was sourced from EM-DAT and then subsetted to look at only 2024. The column "Disaster Type" was used to make a Plotly Express donut chart, with the proportion of each chart segment corresponding to the number of events of that type in 2024.

Significance

Storms and floods were the most prevalent types of natural disaster in 2024, with 147 storm events making up 34.4% of global events, and 143 flood events making up 33.5% of global events. In the next visualization, we will look at the distribution of high-cost natural disaster events in the United States to see differences.

How Does this Distrubution Change when Zooming in to the U.S.?

Hover your cursor over the graph to see the count and percentage for each disaster type.

About the Data

Data was sourced from NOAA and subsetted to only display disasters in 2024. It was then displayed by type of disaster.

Significance

Consistent with the global distribution, storms also make up the majority of natural disasters in the United States. This provides important context for the next page, which will show the distribution of deaths as a result of different types of climate events. Additionally, it lets us set our focus on natural disaster events that are particularly relevant to the United States, providing crucial information for where U.S. policy should be directed.

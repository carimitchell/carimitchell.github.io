How many Lives have been Lost to these Natural Disasters?

Tropical Cyclones caused the Most Deaths in the United States in 2024

About the Data

Data was sourced from NOAA and subsetted to only look at 2024.

Significance

Although we previously learned that severe storms were the most prevalent type of billion-dollar disaster, tropical cyclones caused the most deaths in 2024. This underscores the variability of risk and emphasizes the need for planning based on individual disasters.

There are Many Fluctuations in the Number of Deaths Due to Climate Events in the U.S.

Hover your cursor over the graph to see the exact count for each year.

About the Data

Data was sourced from NOAA and includes data for 1980 through 2024.

Significance

The chart highlights both low-fatality years as well as some spikes in mortality rate across the past forty years. Though many years have relatively few recorded deaths, the data emphasizes how disaster events have the potential to bring varying levels of fatalities. It is important to recognize the deadly nature of these disaster events in order to develop preventative programs and resources.

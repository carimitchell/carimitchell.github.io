Explore the Implications of Climate Change

About our Project

This project examines the impact of climate change on the United States within a global framework, examining how American climate policy fails to align with the data. The United States has resisted many international climate agreements, positioning itself at odds with the global movement toward cooperative action. Examples of Kyoto Protocol and Paris Climate Agreement. Despite this, the nation faces significant environmental, social, and economic consequences from the impact of climate change.

Key Questions

  • How has the number of climate events changed over time?
  • What are the most common natural disasters?
  • How many lives have been lost to these natural disasters?
  • How much do natural disasters cost?

Data Sources

Global climate data was sourced from EM-DAT, a public international disaster database, and NOAA, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Each visualization will explain how the data was processed to create the final product.
